DataBloom.net Consulting

Product, AI, Cloud, and Security – On a Flexible Basis

While my primary goal is a full-time product leadership role, I also work with select companies on a consulting or advisory basis when capacity allows. These engagements are most effective when you need a business + technical hybrid who can ramp quickly, create structure, and help you move from idea to shipped product.

Ways We Can Work Together

1. Product & Strategy Consulting

Short- to medium-term support, typically scoped in weeks or a defined number of hours, focused on:

  • Product strategy, positioning, and roadmap
  • AI/GenAI opportunities and realistic use cases
  • Competitive analysis and differentiation
  • Requirements definition and refinement
  • “Second set of eyes” on strategy, decks, or architecture

2. Interim / Fractional Product Leadership

For teams that need a Product Manager / Product Owner or product leader to fill a gap:

  • Acting PM/PO for a new initiative, product, or business line
  • Interim coverage while you hire a permanent leader
  • Helping early teams put in just enough process to ship reliably

3. Technical & AI Projects

Hands-on work combining product thinking with implementation detail, especially for emerging AI-based systems:

  • LLM prototypes and reference implementations (Python, AWS, Bedrock, LangChain)
  • RAG pipelines, evaluation harnesses, and prompt engineering workflows
  • Secure, cloud-native architectures for AI-powered applications
  • Data analysis and analytics prototypes to validate hypotheses

4. Coaching & Mentorship

For early- to mid-career product professionals, I offer coaching focused on:

  • Navigating product roles and career transitions
  • Handling product/organizational friction and “culture gap” issues
  • Developing a robust, multidimensional product skillset

Profit is not my primary motivator for coaching; I keep pricing accessible and reserve some time for pro bono work.

5. Advisory / Founder Support

I enjoy working with founders at the “pre-everything” stage – before everything is locked in and while the shape of the business is still fluid.

  • Refining value propositions and narratives
  • Thinking through AI, data, and platform strategy
  • Shaping initial roadmap, milestones, and GTM experiments
  • Helping bridge investor, customer, and technical perspectives

Why Work With Me as a Consultant

  • Rare combination of product strategy/management and deep technical capability
  • Experience across AI, cloud, networking, data, and security
  • Ability to create clarity and structure in ambiguous environments
  • Comfortable communicating with engineers, execs, customers, and partners
  • Strong execution bias – I like shipping, not just talking

Engagement Model

Most consulting work is structured as time-and-materials with clearly defined scopes and regular check-ins. For founder advisory roles, compensation may include equity and milestone-based components.

Availability depends on existing commitments; full-time employment takes precedence.
